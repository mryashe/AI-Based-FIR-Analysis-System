Tips for a good description

  • Say what happened, in order.
  • Mention the place and time if you know them.
  • Mention any property taken, damaged, or any injury caused.
  • Mention any threat, force, or false promise used.

From a plain-language story to the right section of law

With BNS replacing the IPC, officers and citizens are still learning the new section numbers. This tool helps bridge that gap at the very first step of a criminal case.

How it works

  1. 1

    Enter the description

    Type or speak the incident in English, Hindi or Marathi.

  2. 2

    Text is cleaned

    NLP preprocessing prepares the text for the AI model.

  3. 3

    AI understands meaning

    The model detects the crime type and key terms in the story.

  4. 4

    Compared with BNS database

    The description is matched against stored BNS sections.

  5. 5

    Sections are ranked

    The most relevant sections are shown with a match score.

  6. 6

    Explanation and FIR draft

    Get a simple explanation, the reason for each match, and an optional draft.

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a decision-support tool. A qualified police officer or legal professional must confirm the final sections.

How does it find the sections?

The description and every BNS section are converted into numerical representations (embeddings). Sections whose meaning is closest to the description are ranked highest.

Can it make mistakes?

Yes. Closely related sections (for example, theft and robbery) can be confused, and a prototype may not cover every section. Always review the suggestions.

Is my text stored?

This page only sends your text to the analysis API configured for it. Whether the text is logged depends on how that backend is deployed.
